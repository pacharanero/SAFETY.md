## Notes for SAFETY.md work

This source is useful for clarifying:

- the split between DCB0129 manufacturer/supplier obligations and DCB0160 deployer/use obligations;
- deployer rights to request DCB0129 Clinical Safety Case Report material;
- the relationship between DCB standards and ISO 14971;
- when DCB standards may apply to Software as a Medical Device implemented within or forming part of a health IT system;
- why ISO 14971 work can be reused as a foundation but does not remove DCB0129/DCB0160 obligations where those standards apply.

The article uses `DCB160` in some headings/body text; this appears to refer to DCB0160. Preserve the upstream wording in source copies, but use `DCB0160` in SAFETY.md-authored interpretation unless quoting.
