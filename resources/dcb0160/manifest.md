## Notes for SAFETY.md work

DCB0160 applies to health and care organisations deploying, using, maintaining, or decommissioning health IT systems. SAFETY.md should make the manufacturer/deployer boundary explicit, especially where a repository is maintained by a supplier but consumed by an NHS or care organisation with separate deployment responsibilities.
