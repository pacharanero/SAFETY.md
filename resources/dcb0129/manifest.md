## Notes for SAFETY.md work

DCB0129 applies to manufacturers of health IT systems. SAFETY.md should map manufacturer-side artefacts such as the Clinical Risk Management Plan, Hazard Log, Clinical Safety Case, and Clinical Risk Management File to repository-native files and GitHub/Git workflows without implying that SAFETY.md replaces the standard itself.
