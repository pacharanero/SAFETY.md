# Licence and re-use

This repository contains several different categories of material. Do not assume that every file is licensed in the same way.

## Original SAFETY.md project material

Unless otherwise stated, original SAFETY.md specification text, commentary, mappings, glossary text, roadmap text, readable reconstructions, examples, and other original material authored for this project by Marcus Baw are licensed under:

**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**

Commercial reuse is allowed and encouraged, but attribution is required and adapted material must be shared under compatible terms.

## Upstream public-sector source material

Files under [`resources/`](./resources/) may include upstream NHS, NHS Digital, NHS England, UK Parliament, legislation.gov.uk, or other public-sector material.

Those files are retained as source/reference material and are governed by their own source licences or re-use terms. Much UK public-sector information is available under the Open Government Licence v3.0, but this must be confirmed per source.

Where known, source URLs, dates accessed, checksums, and licence/re-use notes are recorded in per-folder manifests.

## First-pass renderings of upstream source material

Files under [`upstream/`](./upstream/) are text/Markdown renderings of upstream source files. They are intended to make source material easier to review and map in Git.

These renderings do not convert upstream material into original SAFETY.md-authored material. The underlying upstream licence/re-use terms still apply.

## Legislation

Legislation from legislation.gov.uk is generally available under the Open Government Licence v3.0 except where otherwise stated. Readable reconstructions and briefings authored in this repository are original SAFETY.md project material unless otherwise stated.

## Open licence queries

Outstanding source/licence questions are tracked in [`queries.md`](./queries.md).
