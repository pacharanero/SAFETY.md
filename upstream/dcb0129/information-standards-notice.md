Data Coordination Board
Information Standards Notice – Change

Acting under delegated authority from the Secretary of State for Health and Social
Care, the Data Coordination Board (DCB) has approved a change to an existing
information standard for publication under section 250 of the Health and Social Care
Act 2012.

You must comply with the change to this standard by the full conformance date
specified below.

 Standard details
 DCB unique identifier     DCB0129 Amd 24/2018
 Name                      Clinical Risk Management: its Application in the Manufacture of
                           Health IT Systems, 2018 Update
 Approval date             29 May 2018
 Publication date          7 June 2018
 Standard definition
 Description               This information standard provides a set of requirements
                           suitably structured to promote and ensure the effective
                           application of clinical risk management by those organisations
                           that are responsible for the development and maintenance of
                           Health IT Systems for use within the health and care
                           environment.

                           The standard forms part of the NHS Digital Clinical Safety
                           Standards; it is accompanied by the related standard for the
                           application of clinical risk management in the deployment and
                           use of Health IT Systems (DCB0160).

 About this change
                           Following the publication of the Medical Devices Regulation
                           (EU 2017/745) in May 2017 and evidence of the need for
                           clinical risk assessment of medical devices using software, a
                           decision has been made to extend the scope of the standard to
                           now include medical devices.
                           Previously medical devices have been excluded from the scope
                           of the Clinical Safety Standards.

 Scope (Health and         Health Services, NHS Services, Adult Social Care
 Social Care Act 2012)
 Impacts upon               This Notice is addressed to Manufacturer personnel who are
                            responsible for ensuring clinical safety in the development and
                            modification of Health IT Systems through the application of
                            clinical risk management.

 Legal authority           This information standard is published under section 250 of the
                           Health and Social Care Act 2012.

 Link to further           The Change Specification, Requirements Specification and
