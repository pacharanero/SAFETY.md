# Legislation notes

This folder contains readable reconstructions and practical briefings for legislation relevant to SAFETY.md, clinical safety, health IT, and health/care data use.

It is intentionally separate from [`resources/`](../resources/):

- `resources/` stores upstream source material and provenance.
- `legislation/` explains the practical effect of selected legal changes for SAFETY.md readers.

These notes are **not legal advice**. They are working interpretation notes to help clinical safety officers, software teams, and maintainers understand how legislative changes may affect clinical safety documentation and evidence.

## Method

For each relevant Act or statutory instrument, aim to maintain three layers:

1. **Source/provenance** in `resources/`, with URLs, access dates, and checksums where applicable.
2. **Readable reconstruction** in this folder, explaining what the amended law appears to do in plain language.
3. **Role-specific briefing**, starting with CSO-facing notes, translating the legal effect into practical questions and documentation implications.
