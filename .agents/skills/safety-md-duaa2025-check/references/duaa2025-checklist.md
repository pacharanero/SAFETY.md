# DUAA2025 checklist

- Does the product/service involve health care or adult social care in or in relation to England?
- Is the organisation marketing, supplying, providing, operating, maintaining, or making available IT or an IT service?
- Could applicable information standards cover functionality, connectivity, interoperability, portability, storage, access, or information security?
- Could failures in those domains plausibly contribute to patient harm?
- Is there an evidence pack that could be produced quickly for a compliance query?
- Are owners named for clinical safety, information governance, security, interoperability, operations, and supplier/deployer obligations?
- Are accreditation or future conformance schemes relevant or pending?
