# Review checklist

Apply every item.

## Structure

- Root `SAFETY.md` exists or absence is justified.
- Links to safety case, hazard log, plan, evidence work.
- Version/configuration/release is identifiable.

## Claims

- No unsupported “compliant”, “approved”, “safe”, or “complete” claim.
- Draft/pending status is clear where review is incomplete.
- CSO approval is not invented.

## Roles

- Manufacturer/supplier and deployer/use roles are distinct.
- Operator, maintainer, and relevant IT provider status are explicit or pending.
- If deployment/use is in scope, supplier DCB0129 evidence request/review status is recorded or marked pending.

## Hazards

- Hazards have stable IDs.
- Causes and effects are separated.
- Controls are linked.
- Residual risk is recorded or pending.
- Evidence exists or is marked pending.
- High/unknown residual risks have review/acceptance path.

## ISO 14971 / MDR / DCB relationship

- ISO 14971/MDR evidence is listed as a separate assurance domain where relevant.
- ISO 14971 evidence is not treated as automatically satisfying DCB0129 or DCB0160.
- SaMD/AIaMD cases have MDR triage status or a pending regulatory-review query.
- Deployer evidence records received supplier Clinical Safety Case Report material, transferred controls, residual risks, assumptions, release notes, known issues, and IFU/manual/labelling where relevant.

## DUAA2025

- Applicable standards and assurance domains table present or consciously omitted.
- Interoperability, portability, access, storage, and information-security interfaces considered.
- Adjacent-domain owners are named where relevant.

## Stale references

Flag present-tense or accidental references to NHSX, obsolete NHS Digital ownership, Turva, RCPCH, Digital Growth Charts, Baw Medical, or prior project names. Preserve deliberate attribution/provenance.
